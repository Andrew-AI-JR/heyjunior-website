Setup Guide

Set up Junior in about 5 minutes.

The first time you open Junior, a setup window walks you through six short steps. This page explains each one, in the same order, with fixes if something goes wrong.

See the six steps Something went wrong

Before you start

  • Install Junior (Windows guide / macOS guide) and sign in with your Junior email and password.
  • Make sure Google Chrome is installed.
  • Have your LinkedIn sign-in and your phone nearby, in case LinkedIn asks for a code.

The setup window opens by itself the first time. To open it again later, click Help at the top of Junior, then Run setup again.

The six setup steps

Step 1: Your goal

Choose Find a job (Junior comments on hiring posts that fit you) or Grow my network (Junior comments on posts in your field). You can change this later on the Automation tab.

Commenting as a company page (Enterprise)? Finish setup with your own profile first, then switch to Comment as my company on the Automation tab.

Step 2: Check your computer

Junior checks that Google Chrome and its own engine are installed. Green ticks mean you are ready.

  • Google Chrome not found: install it from google.com/chrome, then click Check again.
  • Junior engine missing: reinstall Junior from your account page, then check again.

Step 3: Connect LinkedIn

  1. Click Open Chrome and sign in.
  2. Sign in to LinkedIn in the Chrome window that opens. If LinkedIn asks for a code or a phone approval, do that too.
  3. Keep Chrome open. When your LinkedIn feed appears, Junior saves your session and closes Chrome by itself, usually within a few seconds.

Junior never sees or stores your LinkedIn password; it keeps only the signed-in session, on your computer. You do this once per computer, unless LinkedIn signs you out.

Step 4: About you

Your bio: 2 to 3 sentences about your role, your experience, and what you are looking for or offering. Junior reads it before writing every comment, so the more specific it is, the more the comments sound like you.

Example: "I am a data engineer with 5 years of experience building pipelines with Python and AWS. I am looking for senior data engineering roles at mid-size companies."

Link (optional): a link Junior can include in your best comments. Job seekers often use a Calendly link; founders and consultants use their website.

You can skip this step, but comments are much better with a bio.

Step 5: What to look for

Search terms are what Junior types into LinkedIn search to find posts to comment on. Click a suggestion or type your own, separated by commas. You need at least one; 5 to 10 work best.

  • Find a job: think like a recruiter posting a job: "hiring product manager", "join our team", "we are hiring remote".
  • Grow my network: topics your audience posts about: "SaaS", "product management", "AI startups".

Step 6: Your first run

Click Start my first run. Junior works in the background and stops after 5 comments, so you can read them on the Activity tab and check they sound like you. Watch progress in Live Activity on the Automation tab, and press Stop at any time.

When you are happy with the comments, press Start on the Automation tab. From then on Junior uses your normal daily limit.

If something goes wrong

Chrome opened and then closed, or never opened

Close every Chrome window and click Open Chrome and sign in again. If Chrome still will not open, restart your computer and try once more.

"LinkedIn was not connected"

Junior closes Chrome itself once you reach your feed. If Chrome was closed first, or sign-in took longer than 5 minutes, click the button and sign in again. Finish any LinkedIn security check you are shown.

Junior says it has nothing to search for

Add at least one search term (step 5), or on the Automation tab under Who & where.

The first run found few posts

Add more search terms, or make them broader. Junior keeps looking until it reaches 5 comments or you press Stop.

LinkedIn signed me out later

Click Help, then Run setup again, and repeat step 3. Or, on the Automation tab, open Account and turn on Open Chrome so you can sign in to LinkedIn again on the next run.

Do I need to sign in every time?

No. Junior reuses your saved session until LinkedIn signs you out.

Still stuck?

In Junior, click Help, then Open log file, and email the file to support@heyjunior.ai. We usually reply the same day.

Using a version before 3.2.24?

Older versions have no setup window. Update Junior when it offers, or set up by hand: on the Automation tab, add your bio and search terms, open Account and turn on Open Chrome so you can sign into LinkedIn, then click Start and sign in to LinkedIn in the Chrome window. Do not close Chrome until Junior closes it.
