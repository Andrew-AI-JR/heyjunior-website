When Junior is a better fit

  • You want AI-assisted comments that sound natural and contextual.
  • You want public conversation visibility before direct messaging.
  • You want LinkedIn automation for job seekers, founders, consultants, or recruiters.

What Junior does today

  • Runs on your own computer, in your browser, with your login and IP address. Your LinkedIn password is never sent to us.
  • Comments in your voice, or as your company page on Enterprise, on posts from the accounts, people, places and topics you choose.
  • Shows its work: a Today page, a live Highlights feed, why each comment matched and why each skipped post was skipped.
  • Shows what every target account and person got you, with suggestions on where to spend your own time.
  • Brings every reply into one inbox with a drafted response, and for job seekers, can Easy Apply to fresh jobs.
