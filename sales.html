FAQ

Sales team questions

Does this guarantee reply rates or meetings?

No. Junior helps your team create better entry points into conversations. Results depend on your market, accounts and settings, and you can see them account by account.

Does it replace our CRM or outbound tools?

No. It complements them by improving the quality of top-of-funnel engagement. Import your Salesforce reports, and export the people who engaged back as Leads.

Can Junior comment as our company page?

Yes, on Enterprise, with admin access to the page. Junior checks the identity on every comment so a brand comment never posts under someone's personal name.

Does Junior need my LinkedIn password?

No. Junior runs on your computer. You sign in to LinkedIn in a browser on your own machine, and your session stays there. See our Privacy Policy.

Is any LinkedIn automation completely risk-free?

No. LinkedIn can restrict automated activity and its rules can change. Junior is designed around local execution, conservative pacing and visible activity, but no product can honestly promise zero risk. See our Terms of Service.
