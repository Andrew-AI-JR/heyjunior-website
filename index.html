How it works

Junior does the daily commenting. Your team takes the conversations.

Show up where buyers already talk, without anyone scrolling LinkedIn for an hour a day.

  1. Tell Junior who matters

    Import target companies and people, or set the industries, topics and search terms your buyers care about.

  2. Junior finds the right posts

    It reads your target accounts' company pages and searches LinkedIn, scoring each post for relevance before it engages.

  3. It comments as you or your brand

    Each comment is written from your background, your goal and the post in front of it, then posted at a human pace.

  4. Replies land in one inbox

    Junior drafts a response to every reply. Send it in a click, let auto-reply handle it, or hand it to a rep.

FAQ

Questions to ask before software touches your LinkedIn

Does Junior need my LinkedIn password?

No. Junior runs on your computer. You sign in to LinkedIn in a browser on your own machine, and your session stays there. See our Privacy Policy.

Can Junior comment as my company page?

Yes, on the Enterprise plan. You need admin access to the LinkedIn company page. Junior checks the identity on every comment so a brand comment never posts under a personal name, and it can still comment as you too.

Will the comments sound generic?

The point is the opposite. Junior writes from your background, your goal and tone, and the specific post. You can try it on a real post before you sign up.

How do I find the right people to engage with?

Import your target companies and people, or give Junior industries, topics and search terms. Role works as a search term too. Junior scores each post for relevance and skips the rest.

What happens when someone replies?

The reply lands in your Conversation Inbox with a drafted response. Send it, edit it, turn on auto-reply, or hand it to a teammate.

What happens after the free trial?

Enterprise is free for 14 days and individual plans for 7. You pay $0 today, and your plan starts at the end of the trial unless you cancel. You can cancel anytime from the billing portal.

What do I need to install?

Junior is a desktop app for Windows 10 or later and macOS (Intel and Apple Silicon). See the setup guide, Windows help and Mac help.

Is any LinkedIn automation completely risk-free?

No. LinkedIn can restrict automated activity and its rules can change. Junior is designed around local execution, conservative pacing and visible activity, but no product can honestly promise zero risk.
