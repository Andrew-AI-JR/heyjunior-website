Your first-day checklist

  1. Download and install. Your computer may show a security warning the first time. That's expected: on Windows click "More info" then "Run anyway"; on Mac right-click Junior and choose "Open". Windows help · Mac help
  2. Sign in to Junior with the email and password you just created.
  3. Connect LinkedIn. Check "2FA", click "Start Automation", and log in to LinkedIn in the Chrome window that opens. Your session stays on your computer.
  4. Tell Junior who matters. Add your bio, goal and search terms, or import your target accounts. Onboarding guide
  5. On Enterprise? Add your company page. Pick the LinkedIn company page you administer so Junior can comment as your brand.
  6. Watch the first comments go out in the Activity tab, then check the Inbox for replies.

Stuck on any step? Email support@heyjunior.ai and the founder will help you get set up, usually the same day.
