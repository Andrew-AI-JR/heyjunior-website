Help: Targets

Target Accounts: comment where it counts.

Tell Junior which companies and people matter most, and it puts their posts first. This page covers adding them, the weekly limits, importing a list, and the suggestions Junior makes.

How Targets work

What does the Targets tab do?

Targets is your priority list. When Junior finds posts from a target company, its people, or a target person, it gives them a boost and comments on them before other posts, within the weekly limits below.

Open it with Targets in the menu at the top of Junior. Each target shows what Junior has done for it so far: comments, replies and other results.

What do Tier 1, 2 and 3 mean?

The tier sets how many comments Junior may post each week across a company and all of its people:

TierComments a week (company and its people together)
Tier 1Up to 7
Tier 2Up to 5
Tier 3Up to 3

Use Tier 1 for the few accounts you care about most.

How often does Junior comment on a target person?

Junior checks a target person's posts once a day and comments on their posts at most once a week. It needs their LinkedIn profile URL to find their posts.

Is my list saved if I change computers?

Yes, if Keep target accounts in my Junior account is on (at the top of the Targets tab). Your list is kept in your Junior account, and each computer keeps a copy, so runs work even without a connection. Sign in on the new computer with the same Junior email and the list comes back.

Adding targets

Add a company

  1. On the Targets tab, open Add or import accounts and people.
  2. Under Add account, fill in the company name and, if you have them, its website domain (for example acme.com) and its LinkedIn company URL. The LinkedIn URL helps Junior find the company's own posts.
  3. Pick a tier, add notes if you like, and click Add account.

Add a person

  1. Under Add person, enter their full name, title and LinkedIn profile URL. The profile URL is required for Junior to find their posts.
  2. Optionally pick a Persona: Recruiter, Hiring manager, Founder / exec, Peer, or Other. The persona shapes the tone of Junior's comments on their posts. Other adds no hint.
  3. Set a Priority from 0 to 100. Higher gives their posts a bigger boost when Junior picks what to comment on.
  4. Link them to one of your target companies under Account, or leave No account, then click Add person.

Import a list (LinkedIn connections or Salesforce)

Under Import CSV, choose a file, click Check file to preview it, then Import.

  • Your LinkedIn connections: on LinkedIn, go to Settings, then Data privacy, then Get a copy of your data, and choose Connections. Pick the Connections.csv file LinkedIn sends you.
  • Salesforce: Accounts, Contacts or Leads reports work. Website becomes the domain, and Rating Hot, Warm or Cold becomes Tier 1, 2 or 3. Save .xlsx reports as CSV first.
  • Your own file: use columns like First Name, Last Name, URL, Company, Position.

Export to Salesforce

Under Export for Salesforce, click Engaged people as Leads or Target accounts & people. Junior saves CSV files on your computer with Salesforce's standard column names, ready for Salesforce's Data Import Wizard (Setup, then Data Import Wizard).

Suggestions and your list

What is “Tune your list”?

Suggestions based on what Junior saw and what came back, such as targets to add, pause or remove. Nothing changes until you press a button. Not now hides a suggestion for 30 days.

Where do the suggested people and companies come from?

  • People who engaged with you: people who replied to Junior's comments, reacted to them, or reacted to target posts Junior commented on.
  • Companies you applied to: companies from your Easy Apply jobs that aren't targets yet. Adding one makes Junior comment on posts by the company and its people. Easy Apply works the same either way.
  • People you've crossed paths with: people at your target companies who replied, reacted, or wrote posts Junior commented on.
  • Local voices: people in your locations that Junior found with LinkedIn people search. They follow their own rules, not the target limits. Remove them, or turn this off, on the Automation tab under Locations.

Nothing happens to any of these until you add them.

Sort or clear the list

Use Sort to order targets by tier, most results, most or fewest comments, or newest or oldest comment. Remove all asks you to confirm first; press Cancel to keep your list.

If something isn't working

Junior hasn't commented on a target yet

  • They haven't posted lately. Junior can only comment on recent posts, and many people post rarely.
  • The weekly limit is used up. The tier limit covers the company and all its people together, and a person gets at most one comment a week.
  • The LinkedIn URL is missing or wrong. Open the target and check the profile or company URL opens the right page on LinkedIn.
  • Junior hasn't run since you added it. Target people are checked once a day while Junior is running.
