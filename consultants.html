How consultants use Junior

FAQ

Consultant questions

Will this sound generic?

No. Junior writes each comment from the post and your positioning, and skips posts it can't add to. Every skipped post is listed with the reason.

Can this replace my outbound strategy?

No. It strengthens your visibility at the top of the funnel while the rest of your sales process stays in place.

How quickly can I start?

Setup takes about five minutes and is built for non-technical users. Junior runs in the desktop app on Windows or Mac.

Can I try it without a credit card?

Yes. Start Junior Basic free for 14 days with just an email and a password. Add a card any time to keep going.

Does Junior need my LinkedIn password?

No. Junior runs on your computer. You sign in to LinkedIn in a browser on your own machine, and your session stays there. See our Privacy Policy.
