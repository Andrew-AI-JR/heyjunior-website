See Junior 3.0 in action

Operator Console, Activity, Run Insights, and company-page commenting — in under a minute.

Operator Console

The Automation screen is now the daily workspace instead of a settings page you visit once.

  • Targeting, filters, and caps stay visible while a run is in progress. Nothing load-bearing is hidden behind a "simple mode".
  • A run bar summarises the active identity and run state.
  • An agent header reports one of four honest states: idle, needs you, working, problem. "Needs you" is a real pause, such as a login or MFA prompt, not a guess.
  • The raw log is still there as a drawer. It was never removed.

Activity

A dedicated third screen for what Junior actually did, replacing the cramped Recent Activity box on the Dashboard.

  • Filter by posted, skipped, or all. It defaults to posted.
  • Skipped entries carry the reason, so an empty run is explainable instead of mysterious.
  • Posted comments open on LinkedIn in one click.

Run Insights

A "Review latest run" card on the Dashboard. You press it, Junior reviews the most recent session's own telemetry, and it reports what your configuration actually produced.

  • Manual only. It does not run in the background or phone home between runs.
  • Rate limited to 3 reviews per day.

Comment as your company page (Enterprise)

Junior can post as your LinkedIn company page instead of your personal profile.

  • Entitlement is fail-closed: if the check fails or the answer is unknown, company commenting stays locked rather than silently falling back to your personal profile.
  • Identity is frozen mid-run, so a plan change can never switch who is posting halfway through.
  • Saved company details stay visible and read-only when the feature is locked.

Find posts from company pages

Point Junior at specific LinkedIn company pages and it harvests their official feeds alongside your keyword searches.

  • URL-only field. There is no limit on how many pages you add; a longer list just takes longer to cycle.
  • Distinct from "Author companies", which filters by where a post's author works.
  • Per-author cooldown is skipped on official company feeds, so an active page is not throttled to one comment.

Better post selection and counting

  • Posts older than 10 days are no longer eligible, so Junior stops commenting on stale threads.
  • Comment action bars are no longer mistaken for posts during harvesting.
  • Today's comment count survives an app restart, and a separate session counter shows the current run.
  • Counts read from the real Documents path rather than a OneDrive-redirected copy.

Reliability

  • A crashed company-feed pass no longer loses comments that were already posted, and leftover pages are picked up on restart rather than silently skipped.
  • Expired API tokens refresh mid-run instead of ending the session.
  • The LinkedIn session is restored from the Junior Chrome profile, reducing repeat manual logins.
  • Fixes for an installer crash, a stalled-run restart loop, and an unsolvable manual-login captcha.
