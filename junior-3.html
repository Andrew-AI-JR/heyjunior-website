What 3.0 gives you

Same job as before: Junior comments on the right LinkedIn posts for you. These four changes make it clearer what it is doing — and safer to run for work.

Watch Junior work while it runs

You can see who Junior is targeting and what it is doing right now, instead of starting a run and hoping for the best.

  • Your targeting stays on screen while Junior is working.
  • A simple status tells you if Junior is idle, working, needs you (for example a login), or hit a problem.
  • The full activity log is still there if you want it.

See every comment it posted

A dedicated Activity screen shows what Junior actually did, not a cramped list on the dashboard.

  • Filter by posted or skipped. It starts on posted.
  • If Junior skipped a post, you see why — so an empty run is explainable.
  • Open any posted comment on LinkedIn in one click.

Get a recap of the last session

Press “Review latest run” on the dashboard and Junior summarizes what that session produced.

  • You ask for it. It does not run in the background.
  • Limited to 3 reviews per day.

Comment as your company, not just your profile

On Enterprise, Junior can post as your LinkedIn company page. That is the plan for people who comment on LinkedIn for the business, not only themselves.

  • If Junior cannot confirm you have Enterprise, company commenting stays locked. It will not quietly post as you instead.
  • Once a run starts, the identity stays put — a plan change cannot switch who is posting halfway through.

What’s under the hood

For people who want the product details. Skip this if you already know you want Junior to comment for you.

Find posts from company pages

  • Point Junior at specific LinkedIn company pages and it reads their official feeds alongside your keyword searches.
  • This is different from “Author companies,” which filters by where a post’s author works.
  • Official company feeds are not limited to one comment per author, so an active page is not throttled.

Better post selection and counting

  • Posts older than 10 days are skipped, so Junior does not comment on stale threads.
  • Comment action bars are no longer mistaken for posts.
  • Today’s comment count survives an app restart, and a separate counter shows the current run.

Reliability

  • A crashed company-feed pass no longer loses comments that were already posted.
  • Expired API tokens refresh mid-run instead of ending the session.
  • The LinkedIn session is restored from the Junior Chrome profile, so you log in less often.
  • Fixes for an installer crash, a stalled-run restart loop, and a manual-login captcha that could not be solved.
