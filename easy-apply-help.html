Help: Easy Apply

Easy Apply: fresh jobs, between comments.

If your goal is Find a job, Junior can apply to new LinkedIn Easy Apply jobs for you, using only answers you give it. It starts in dry run, so nothing is sent until you say so.

How Easy Apply works

What does Easy Apply do?

Between comments, Junior searches LinkedIn Jobs for your search terms (and your job locations, if you set them). It looks at Easy Apply jobs only, newest first, and fills in each application from your answers.

It only works with LinkedIn Easy Apply. Junior doesn't fill in applications on company career sites.

Where is it? I don't see Applications

Easy Apply is part of the Find a job goal. When that's your goal, Applications appears in the menu at the top of Junior. To change your goal, click Help, then Run setup again, or pick it on the Automation tab.

Turn it on

  1. Open Applications.
  2. Turn on Easy Apply to fresh jobs. It starts in Dry run.
  3. Fill in Your answers so fewer jobs wait on you.

Dry run versus actually submitting

Dry run (the default): Junior fills each application up to LinkedIn's Review step, then discards it. Nothing is sent. These show as Filled to Review (not sent), so you can check how Junior would answer.

Actually submit applications: when you turn this on, Junior asks you to confirm, because it will send real applications to employers under your name. Then it clicks Submit after filling each form, at most 3 a day in the first week. It never applies to a company you applied to in the last 30 days.

Settings

How many, when, and which jobs

  • Applications a day: 1 to 25 (10 to start).
  • Jobs posted in the last: from 30 minutes to a week. Fresh jobs get fewer applicants.
  • Between: the hours Junior may apply.
  • Skip jobs with more applicants than: 100 to start.
  • Weekdays only and Follow the company when applying.
  • Never apply to these companies: for your current employer, or anywhere you'd rather not apply.

Your answers: what Junior uses

Junior only uses answers you give it, under Your answers: work eligibility (authorized to work, visa sponsorship, relocation, on-site or hybrid), experience (total years, years per skill, highest degree), pay and start date (salary, OTE, hourly rate, notice period), security clearance, your location, and contact links (phone, LinkedIn, GitHub, website).

Leave anything blank you'd rather answer case by case.

Questions waiting for you

If a required question has no answer, Junior never guesses. It skips that job, and the question waits at the top of the Applications page. Answer it once and Junior uses your answer from the next Easy Apply window. The ones you've answered are listed under Questions you've answered.

Results

What do the statuses mean?

StatusMeaning
AppliedJunior submitted the application.
Filled to Review (not sent)Dry run: filled in, then discarded.
Waiting for your answerA required question needs your answer first.
Couldn't finishSomething on LinkedIn stopped the application. Nothing was sent.
SkippedThe job didn't fit your settings, or you applied to that company recently.

See them under Recent applications, filter by status, and archive the ones you're done with. Press Refresh to update the list.

Companies I applied to show up in Targets

Yes. Under Companies you applied to on the Targets tab, add one and Junior also comments on posts by that company and its people, which helps your application get noticed. See Targets suggestions.

If something isn't working

Junior isn't applying to anything

  • Check Easy Apply to fresh jobs is on, and that it's within your Between hours (and a weekday, if Weekdays only is on).
  • Look for questions waiting at the top of Applications. Unanswered required questions make Junior skip jobs.
  • Widen Jobs posted in the last or raise Skip jobs with more applicants than. Very tight settings can leave no jobs.
  • Remember dry run sends nothing. Turn on Actually submit applications to send real applications.
  • Check you haven't reached Applications a day, or the 3-a-day limit in your first week of submitting.

Still stuck?

Ask the help assistant, check Troubleshooting, or email support@heyjunior.ai with a screenshot. In Junior, Help, then Open log file, gives us the details we need.
