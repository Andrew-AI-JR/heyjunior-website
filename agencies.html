How agencies use Junior

One client, one computer, one voice

Each client account uses its own Junior subscription on its own machine, so sessions, IP addresses and browsers never mix.

  1. Set up each client

    Install Junior on the client's computer, sign in to their LinkedIn there, and add their bio, goal and tone.

  2. Load their targets

    Import the client's target accounts and people from CSV or Salesforce, or set the topics and roles they care about.

  3. Junior comments as them

    Each comment is written for the post from the client's positioning, as the client or their company page, at a human pace.

  4. Show what it got them

    Replies land in one inbox per client, and every target shows its results, so client reporting is easy.

FAQ

Agency questions

Can we run Junior from our office for all our clients?

Junior is designed to run on each client's own computer. That keeps IP addresses, sessions and browsers separate for every account, which is safer than running many accounts from one place.

Do you offer agency pricing?

Each client account uses a standard subscription. Email us to talk about volume.

Can we show clients what Junior did?

Yes. Every target shows its results, the Activity log lists every posted and skipped post with the reason, and the dashboards count only comments LinkedIn verified.

Will LinkedIn detect this?

Junior runs locally and paces itself like a person, but every third-party tool carries platform risk. We design for low risk and are honest that no tool can promise zero.

Does Junior need my LinkedIn password?

No. Junior runs on your computer. You sign in to LinkedIn in a browser on your own machine, and your session stays there. See our Privacy Policy.
