How it works

Set it up once. Junior keeps you visible every day.

About five minutes of setup, then Junior works in the desktop app while you do your actual job.

  1. Tell Junior about you

    Your background, your goal (clients, customers, partners, a new role) and the tone you write in.

  2. Pick who matters

    Add the people and companies you want to reach, or the industries, topics and roles you care about.

  3. Junior comments as you

    It scores each post for relevance, skips the rest, and writes a comment for that specific post, at a human pace.

  4. You take the replies

    Replies land in one inbox with a drafted response. Send it, edit it, or turn on auto-reply.

Replies inbox

Never lose a reply again

Every reply and mention lands in one inbox, grouped by post and person, with the next step already drafted.

  • AI-drafted replies that use the original post and the full thread.
  • Optional auto-reply for busy days.
  • Snooze a thread and it comes back when the person replies again.
  • Run Insights shows what Junior engaged with, what it skipped and why.
