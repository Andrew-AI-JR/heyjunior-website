FAQ

Founder questions

Will this replace my voice?

No. Junior writes from your background, point of view and tone, and you can see and change every setting. It supports your judgment rather than replacing it.

Does this guarantee meetings?

No. It makes your presence more consistent and relevant so more good conversations can start. You can see what each target got you.

Is setup heavy?

No. It takes about five minutes, then Junior runs in the desktop app on Windows or Mac.

Can I try it without a credit card?

Yes. Start Junior Basic free for 14 days with just an email and a password. Add a card any time to keep going.

Does Junior need my LinkedIn password?

No. Junior runs on your computer. You sign in to LinkedIn in a browser on your own machine, and your session stays there. See our Privacy Policy.
