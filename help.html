Still stuck?

Email support@heyjunior.ai. We usually reply within one business day. To get a fix in one reply, include:

  1. Windows or Mac.
  2. What you tried, and what happened instead.
  3. A screenshot, or the log file: click Help at the top of Junior, then Open log file.
