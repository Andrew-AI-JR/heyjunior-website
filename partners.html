Questions

How much do I earn?

20% of every payment from customers who sign up through your link, after payment-processing fees and taxes. It keeps coming with every payment for as long as they stay a customer.

When do I get paid?

With Stripe, your share is sent as each customer payment comes in. In countries Stripe doesn't cover, we pay through Wise every Monday once you've earned at least $20 (Wise's fee comes out of the payment).

How do you know a customer came from me?

When someone opens your partner link, we remember it for 30 days. If they sign up in that time, they're linked to you for good.

Who can join? Is there a cost?

Anyone with a Junior account, and it's free. There's no minimum and no contract.

Do I have to use Junior myself?

No, but it helps. Your free month of Pro lets you see exactly what your clients will get.
