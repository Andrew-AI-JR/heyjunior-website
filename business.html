FAQ

Questions teams ask before they start

What do I need to comment as our company page?

Admin access to the LinkedIn company page, the Enterprise plan and the Junior desktop app. Junior checks the identity on every comment so a brand comment never posts under a personal name, and it can still comment as you too.

Does Junior need our LinkedIn password?

No. Junior runs on your computer. You sign in to LinkedIn in a browser on your own machine, and your session stays there. See our Privacy Policy.

Do we approve comments before they post?

No. Junior posts automatically within your settings and the daily limit you choose. Every posted and skipped comment shows up in the Activity tab with a link to LinkedIn, so you can review it and edit or delete it there.

Is any LinkedIn automation completely risk-free?

No. LinkedIn can restrict automated activity and its rules can change. Junior is designed around local execution, conservative pacing and visible activity, but no product can honestly promise zero risk. See our Terms of Service.
