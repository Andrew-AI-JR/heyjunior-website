How it works

Get seen where hiring actually happens

Many roles get filled through conversations before or alongside the job post. Junior puts you in those threads every day, without hours of scrolling.

  1. Tell Junior what you want

    Add your background, the roles you're after and the companies you'd love to work for.

  2. Junior finds hiring posts

    It looks for posts from recruiters, hiring managers and teams in your field, and skips anything that isn't relevant.

  3. It comments as you

    Each comment is written from your experience for that specific post, so it sounds like you, not a template.

  4. You take the conversation

    Replies land in one inbox with a drafted response, so you can move a promising thread toward an interview.

FAQ

Common questions from job seekers

Will this guarantee interviews?

No tool can guarantee interviews. Junior helps you start more relevant conversations with recruiters and hiring managers, which can lead to better opportunities.

Does this replace applications?

No. It works alongside your applications by making you visible in the hiring conversations happening on LinkedIn.

Which plan should I pick?

Start with Basic. Ten thoughtful comments a day is plenty for most searches. Move to Starter if you're targeting a lot of companies or roles at once.

How fast can I start?

Setup takes a few minutes in the desktop app. After that, Junior starts finding posts and commenting the same day. See the setup guide.

Do I need to keep my computer on?

Yes, while Junior is working. It runs in the desktop app, so it only comments while the app is open and the computer is awake.
