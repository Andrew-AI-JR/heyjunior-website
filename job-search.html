How it works

Get seen where hiring actually happens

Many roles get filled through conversations before or alongside the job post. Junior puts you in those threads every day, without hours of scrolling.

  1. Tell Junior what you want

    Add your background, the roles you're after, where you want to work (on-site, hybrid or remote, within a radius you set) and the companies you'd love to work for.

  2. Junior finds hiring posts

    It looks for real openings posted by the employer's own recruiters, hiring managers and teams, judged by where the job is. Staffing agencies, reposted jobs and anything off-topic are skipped.

  3. It comments as you

    Each comment is written from your experience for that specific post, so it sounds like you, not a template.

  4. You take the conversation

    Replies land in one inbox with a drafted response, so you can move a promising thread toward an interview.

Easy Apply

Apply to fresh jobs between comments

Optional and off until you turn it on. Junior searches LinkedIn Jobs for the roles and places you set, Easy Apply jobs only, newest first, and fills in each application from answers you saved.

Fresh jobs that fit

By default Junior only opens jobs posted in the last hour and skips jobs with more than 100 applicants. Titles and places that don't match your search are skipped too.

Your answers, never guesses

Save your answers once: work authorization, sponsorship, years of experience, degree, pay and start date. If a required question has no saved answer, Junior skips that job and the question waits for you. Answer it once and Junior goes back to the jobs that were waiting on it.

Dry run until you say so

Until you turn on submitting, Junior fills each application up to Review, saves a screenshot and discards it. Nothing is sent. Once on, it submits at most 3 a day in the first week.

Paced, with a daily limit

A few applications at a time, only in the hours you choose, up to your daily limit (10 by default, 25 at most). If LinkedIn shows a limit or a warning, Junior stops applying for the day.

Companies you skip

Junior never applies to companies on your block list, such as your current employer, to a company you applied to in the last 30 days, or to a job you already applied to.

Applications next to your targets

Each job is listed once with its latest status: applied, filled to Review, waiting for your answer, skipped or couldn't finish. Jobs at the companies you're targeting show up next to those companies, with what Junior's comments got you there.

What it doesn't do: Junior only handles LinkedIn Easy Apply, not applications on company websites. It sends the resume LinkedIn selects for you and doesn't write cover letters. Demographic questions get "decline to answer" when that option is offered. Easy Apply runs in the desktop app when Junior comments as you on hiring posts, and it's included on Basic, Starter and the 14-day trial.

FAQ

Common questions from job seekers

Will this guarantee interviews?

No tool can guarantee interviews. Junior helps you start more relevant conversations with recruiters and hiring managers, which can lead to better opportunities.

Does this replace applications?

No. Commenting works alongside your applications by making you visible in the hiring conversations happening on LinkedIn. If you turn on Easy Apply, Junior can also fill in LinkedIn Easy Apply applications for you.

Can Junior apply to jobs for me?

Yes, for LinkedIn Easy Apply jobs, if you turn it on. Junior fills each application from answers you saved and never guesses a required question: that job is skipped and the question waits for you. Until you turn on submitting, Junior stops at the Review page and sends nothing. It's included on every plan that comments as you, including the 14-day trial.

Which plan should I pick?

Start with Basic. Ten thoughtful comments a day is plenty for most searches. Move to Starter if you're targeting a lot of companies or roles at once.

How fast can I start?

Setup takes a few minutes in the desktop app. After that, Junior starts finding posts and commenting the same day. See the setup guide.

Do I need to keep my computer on?

Yes, while Junior is working. It runs in the desktop app, so it only comments and applies while the app is open and the computer is awake.
