Help: Inbox

Inbox: keep the conversation going.

When people reply to Junior's comments, their replies land in the Inbox. Junior can draft an answer for you to review and send. This page explains how it works and what each status means.

How the Inbox works

What shows up in the Inbox?

Every comment Junior has confirmed on LinkedIn becomes a conversation in the Inbox. When someone replies to one of your comments, the conversation moves up so you can answer it. Reactions are tracked too, but a reaction alone doesn't need an answer.

Open it with Inbox in the menu at the top of Junior.

How often does Junior check for replies?

Replies to your personal comments are checked about every 6 hours while Junior is running. Mentions of your company are checked once when a company run starts. Junior has to be open and running to check.

What do Needs Reply, Active, Waiting and Closed mean?

TabWhat's in it
Needs ReplySomeone replied to your comment and it's your turn.
ActiveJunior's confirmed comments, with no reply to answer yet. Reactions stay here.
WaitingYou answered and are waiting to hear back.
ClosedFinished conversations.
High priority, ConvertedConversations you marked. Click the button again to unmark one.

Replying

Draft and send a reply

  1. Open a conversation under Needs Reply.
  2. Click Auto-reply (or Draft reply). Junior writes a draft in your voice. It shows Drafting… while it works.
  3. Read the draft. If it's right, click Send reply. If not, click Cancel.

Junior posts the reply the next time it starts, or at the end of the current batch if it's already running. You don't have to wait for the 6-hour reply check.

Will Junior ever post a reply I didn't approve?

No. A reply goes out only after you press Send reply, and Junior checks again just before posting. It won't post if the draft has a safety flag, if the conversation now needs a person, or if the person opted out or moved the conversation off LinkedIn. Junior replies at most once in a thread on its own.

What are Handoffs?

Conversations Junior thinks you should handle yourself are marked Step in, Stop or Check external. They collect under the Handoffs filter until you acknowledge them (press A). For example: the person wants to talk by email or phone, pushed back more than once, or the thread isn't a good fit.

“Could not send”: why a reply didn't post

The conversation card says what happened. The most common reasons:

  • Junior couldn't open the reply box, or LinkedIn kept the Reply button disabled. Nothing was sent.
  • The post is no longer available, or Junior couldn't find its original comment.
  • The browser closed before the reply was sent.
  • Junior ran out of time in that run.

To try again, review the draft and press Auto-reply again. If the post is gone, there's nothing to reply to.

Filters and shortcuts

Filters

  • Incomplete replies: Junior couldn't capture the whole reply. Open the post to read it.
  • Target accounts: conversations with your target companies and people.
  • Handoffs: conversations waiting for you to step in.
  • Auto-reply ready: replies Junior can draft an answer to now, with a count.

Put a conversation aside (Wait)

Choose 4h, 24h or 3d next to Wait, then press W on a conversation to hide it for that long. Press R to bring it back early.

Keyboard shortcuts

Press ? in the Inbox to see them. / search · 1–6 tabs · I incomplete · T targets · H handoffs · O open post · P profile · L copy link · W wait · R resume · A acknowledge · Esc clear.

If something isn't working

The Inbox is empty

Conversations appear after Junior has confirmed a comment on LinkedIn or someone replies. If Junior hasn't posted yet, start with the setup guide. If it has, keep Junior running: replies are checked about every 6 hours.

Someone replied but it isn't in the Inbox

Junior reads replies from your LinkedIn notifications while it runs. Give it up to 6 hours of running time. If the reply still doesn't appear, email support with the post link.

Still stuck?

Ask the help assistant, check Troubleshooting, or email support@heyjunior.ai with a screenshot. In Junior, Help, then Open log file, gives us the details we need.
