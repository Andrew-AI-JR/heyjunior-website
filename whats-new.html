What's new

What's new in Junior

Every release since Junior 3.0, in plain words. The desktop app updates itself, so you already have the latest version. Download it here if you're new.

November 8–9, 2026

3.2.17–3.2.19 · Targets show what they got you

  • Every target account and person shows its results: comments, replies, people who engaged, and a roll-up across all your targets.
  • A plain line on what Junior is doing for each target right now, and a funnel and weekly strips that show how far each one has got.
  • Effort against results, with suggestions on which targets to keep and which to drop.
  • A short brief on a person before you follow up yourself, and each target's timeline told as a story.
  • Nudges on your Today page and a notification when a target replies. Inbox conversations are tied to their target account and person.
  • Job seekers see their applications next to their target companies, and Local voices appear in Targets, labelled.
November 5–8, 2026

3.2.11–3.2.16 · Easy Apply finds more and repeats less

  • Once you answer a question an application was waiting on, Junior goes back to those jobs.
  • Easy Apply reads more pages of results, finds more jobs, and misreads fewer LinkedIn limit messages.
  • Each job is listed once, with its latest status.
  • Junior no longer reopens posts it has already checked, and slow reply drafts are no longer dropped.
November 2–3, 2026

3.2.4–3.2.10 · Real job openings, and safer data

  • "Employers' own openings only": staffing-agency and reposted job posts are skipped, and job posts are judged by where the job is.
  • When a trial ends, Junior stops and says so, and "Choose a plan" opens your account already signed in.
  • A one-time catch-up brings replies you missed into the Inbox. Replies work again with LinkedIn's new notifications layout.
  • A failed read can no longer wipe your Inbox or your target accounts.
October 30, 2026

3.2.0 · A redesign around what you want

  • Start by picking a goal: find a job, grow my network, or comment as my company. Junior shows only the settings that goal uses.
  • A new Today page with what needs you, your replies and how the last run went, plus a first-run checklist.
  • A status that always gives a reason, and a live Highlights feed of setup and progress.
  • Settings in tabs that save themselves, and a new Applications tab for Easy Apply.
October 2026

3.1.53–3.1.75 · Easy Apply, Local voices and job locations

  • Easy Apply: Junior fills LinkedIn Easy Apply applications from answers you saved, never guesses a required question, and submits only once you turn submitting on.
  • Job locations: choose where the jobs are, with a radius and on-site, hybrid or remote.
  • Local voices: find people posting in your city, state or region and comment on their posts.
  • A search allowance with daily and hourly limits, fewer searches at night, and a full stop if LinkedIn pauses search on your account.
  • Run Insights can now suggest a change to any setting, with the evidence behind it.
  • Junior reopens itself and resumes after a crash.
September 2026

3.1 · Replies, people and accounts

  • Inbox drafts use the whole thread, and auto-reply sends a reviewed reply during a run.
  • People who engaged with you: repliers and people who reacted, suggested as targets. People you've crossed paths with at your target accounts.
  • Salesforce CSV import and export, and target accounts kept in your Junior account.
  • Pick company pages to follow by name, and a writing style and networking goal for your comments.
  • Fewer LinkedIn page loads for each comment.
September 3, 2026

3.0 · Company pages and Run Insights

  • Comment as your LinkedIn company page on Enterprise, with an identity check on every comment.
  • Find posts from the company pages you choose, alongside your keyword searches.
  • An Activity screen with every posted and skipped post, and the reason for each skip.
  • Run Insights reviews your latest run and reports what your settings produced.
